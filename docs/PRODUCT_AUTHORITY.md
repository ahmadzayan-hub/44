# Product Authority — Project 44 / RailMind Agent OS

Status: **canonical foundation**

## Primary users

Asset owner, rail maintenance department, contract manager, maintenance contractor, operator, reliability engineer and executive decision-maker.

## Job to be done

Provide one governed, evidence-backed operating environment that answers:

**What is happening across asset, maintenance, contract and financial performance; what requires attention; why; and what decision is needed now?**

## System-of-record boundary

Project 44 does not replace authoritative source systems.

- IBM Maximo remains authoritative for asset register, work orders, PM and maintenance history.
- Finance workflow remains authoritative for invoices, approvals and payment state.
- Approved contract documents remain authoritative for obligations, clauses, KPI formulas and evidence rules.
- Operational / signalling systems remain authoritative for rail control.

Project 44 owns the cross-domain decision layer: evidence references, deterministic KPI results where explicitly assigned, exceptions, AI analysis, recommendations, reviews, approvals, report packages and decision outcomes.

## Canonical capabilities

- Agent orchestration and policy enforcement
- Evidence and provenance model
- Asset and maintenance decision intelligence
- Cross-domain exception management
- Contract/KPI context retrieval
- Finance context retrieval
- Monthly, quarterly and annual reporting intelligence
- Executive briefings
- Decision memory and audit trail
- Human approval gates

## Human decision boundary

AI recommends, explains and drafts. Humans approve high-impact contractual, financial and safety-related outputs. Project 44 performs no autonomous safety-critical rail control action.

## North-star outcome

**Reduced decision latency with better evidence, fewer reporting handoffs and earlier intervention on material asset or contract risk.**
